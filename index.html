🛡️ 1. Privacy Policy & Data Collection Disclosures

Core Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how LifeOS ("we", "our", or "the application") collects, stores, and protects user information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

A. Information Handled by the Application

LifeOS operates primarily as an Offline-First & Local-First Application. Unlike cloud SaaS products that store your private business deals on remote multi-tenant servers, LifeOS saves your records directly to your device's private SQLite storage:

  • Tasks & Routines: Task titles, priority levels (High/Medium/Low), workspace category (Business vs. Personal), and completion status.
  • CRM & Sales Pipeline: Client company names, contact phone/email, deal valuation, sales stage (Discovery, Proposal, Closed Won), and interaction notes.
  • Financial Ledgers: Transaction amounts, GST/tax categorization, cashflow type (Income / Expense), and payment dates.
  • Strategic Memory Vault: Private encrypted notes and operational strategies.

B. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

We have ZERO third-party advertising SDKs, ZERO tracking beacons, and ZERO data broker telemetry. We do not integrate Facebook Pixel, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or any ad networks. Your financial transactions and CRM client leads are strictly confidential to you.

Artificial Intelligence (Gemini Integration):
AI features (such as schedule optimization or routine analysis) are strictly user-initiated. When you invoke AI analysis, only the specific query text is transmitted over TLS 1.3 encryption to the Google Gemini API. Your entire SQLite database is never uploaded or used for model training.

🔑 2. Device Permissions & Hardware Utilization

Play Store Disclosures

LifeOS adheres to the principle of Least-Privilege Permissions. We only request permissions necessary to execute core user-demanded functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used solely to alert you of scheduled task reminders, habit check-ins, and daily business briefings that you have explicitly created.
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to trigger timely task reminders and morning routine alarms at the precise minute requested by the user.
  • VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during stopwatch sessions, pomodoro completions, and urgent priority alerts.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Leverages Android's Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) and BiometricPrompt API for optional app-lock. LifeOS never accesses or stores your raw fingerprints or biometric templates.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly to verify Google Play In-App Billing subscriptions and user-triggered Gemini AI requests.

📦 3. Data Portability & How to Export Your Data

Zero Lock-In

Under Section 12 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and GDPR Article 20, you hold an absolute right to export your complete records in structured, machine-readable formats.

How to Export Directly from the LifeOS Mobile App:
1. Open the LifeOS Android App.
2. Navigate to Settings → Data Sovereignty & Privacy.
3. Tap "Export Complete Workspace".
4. Select your preferred format: JSON (for backups and cross-platform import) or CSV / Excel (for accounting and tax reporting).
5. The file is saved directly to your device's Downloads folder or shared via Android's native share sheet to your Google Drive.

Because LifeOS does not store your records on centralized remote cloud servers, the most comprehensive and immediate export is always generated directly on your device via the steps above.
