1. DPDP Act 2023 Statutory Disclosures

Statutory Notice

LifeOS operates in strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Under the law, LifeOS acts as a Data Fiduciary adhering to purpose limitation and minimal data processing.

Your Rights under DPDP Act 2023:
  • Section 11: Right to access summary of processed personal data.
  • Section 12: Right to correction, erasure, and full data portability (JSON/CSV export).
  • Section 13: Direct Grievance Redressal Officer contact: goyalaryan906@gmail.com.

2. Privacy Policy & Local-First Architecture

Zero Broker

LifeOS stores your operational data (Tasks, CRM Leads, GST Invoices, and Memory Vault) on your local device hardware using SQLite encryption.

  • No Advertising Trackers: We do not track you across other apps or sell data to third-party ad networks.
  • Biometric Privacy: Biometric authentication is handled by Android's hardware keystore; LifeOS never sees or transmits raw biometric signatures.

3. AI & Google Gemini Policy

AI Policy

AI features use Google Gemini API with token limits and selective user prompts only.

  • Your database is never uploaded to the cloud in bulk.
  • API calls are strictly ephemeral and are not used to train foundational AI models.
