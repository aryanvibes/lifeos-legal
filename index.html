⚖️ Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

Statutory Notice

LifeOS operates in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), enacted by the Parliament of India. As a Data Fiduciary, we uphold statutory consumer rights:

  • Section 6 (Purpose Limitation): Data collected is processed solely for workspace management and client CRM as consented by the user.
  • Section 11 (Right to Access): Right to obtain confirmation and intelligible summaries of personal data processed.
  • Section 12 (Right to Correction & Erasure): Right to rectify inaccurate records or order immediate, permanent data wiping.

🔒 Privacy Architecture & Data Handling

Zero Data Brokers

Unlike conventional SaaS platforms that monetize user data through advertising exchanges, LifeOS is engineered with a strict Local-First Architecture:

  • Isolated Room SQLite Database: Your task descriptions, client contacts, deal valuations, and GST billing records live in the private sandbox on your phone hardware.
  • Hardware Biometrics: Fingerprint and PIN authentication are processed by Android's Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). LifeOS never accesses raw biometric fingerprints.
  • Selective AI Processing: Calls to Google Gemini are only executed when explicitly invoked by the user for routine analysis. Raw databases are never transmitted.
