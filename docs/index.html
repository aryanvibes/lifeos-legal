AI Ethics

How LifeOS Processes AI Queries:

❓ 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Verified Statutory Answers

Clear, transparent answers regarding LifeOS local storage, Omni AI data handling, DPDP Act 2023 compliance, billing terms, and identity erasure.

📱 Is my personal data stored on LifeOS cloud servers or on my smartphone?
▼

100% on your smartphone. LifeOS is built with a Local-First, Zero-Trust architecture. Your tasks, daily routines, client pipelines, ledgers, tax invoices, and memory notes are stored in your device's internal private SQLite sandbox (/data/data/com.lifeos.android/databases/). They are never synchronized to our servers. Only your Google account email/UID and subscription license tier are maintained on Firebase to authenticate access and prevent abuse.

🤖 Does Omni AI train on my personal notes, tasks, or financial entries?
▼

No. LifeOS executes deterministic operations (such as scheduling calculations, date parsing, and local searches) on-device without internet access. When you invoke Omni AI for natural language summarization or workflow generation, only the explicit query text is sent over TLS 1.3 to secure Omni AI processing servers. Your private SQLite database is never uploaded, and under enterprise terms, queries are not retained to train public machine learning models.

🗑️ How can I permanently delete my account and erase all associated data?
▼

LifeOS provides total identity sovereignty:
• To delete your cloud license & Google profile: Sign in with Google in Section 1 of this Trust Centre, type DELETE, and confirm. Your record is purged immediately from Firebase servers.
• To erase local device data: Open LifeOS App → Settings → Data Sovereignty → Clear All Data, or simply uninstall the app from your Android device.

⚖️ How does LifeOS comply with India's DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025?
▼

LifeOS functions as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. We provide clear notice (Section 6), complete access to your records (Section 11), immediate self-service erasure (Section 12), designated Grievance Redressal within 24–72 hours (Section 13), and the statutory right to nominate a representative (Section 14).

🔑 What Android permissions does LifeOS require and why?
▼

LifeOS strictly adheres to the principle of Least Privilege. We only request:
• POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): To deliver routine alerts and task reminders you schedule.
• SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To ensure notifications trigger at the precise minute.
• VIBRATE: For haptic feedback on focus timers.
• USE_BIOMETRIC: To unlock your app with fingerprint authentication inside the device Hardware TEE.
We never request camera, contacts, microphone, photo storage, or background location permissions.

💳 How do Razorpay subscriptions, renewals, and refunds work?

Optional premium plans (Starter: ₹499/mo, Pro: ₹999/mo, Master: ₹2,499/mo) are billed via Razorpay in INR. Subscriptions can be canceled at any time in-app or via support email without penalty. Under Indian Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, duplicate debits or technical provisioning failures reported within 7 business days receive a 100% refund credited back within 5–7 banking days.

📦 Can I export my data to take it with me or use in spreadsheets?
▼

Yes, zero vendor lock-in. You can export your complete workspace in open JSON (for automated backups) or CSV (for Excel, Google Sheets, or CA tax audits) by going to LifeOS App → Settings → Data Sovereignty & Privacy → Export Complete Workspace. The file is saved directly to your phone's Downloads directory.
