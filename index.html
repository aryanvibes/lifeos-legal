Statutory Notice

LifeOS complies with the provisions of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. As a registered Data Fiduciary, we uphold the highest statutory obligations for your digital privacy:

  • Section 11 (Right to Access): Right to obtain an intelligible summary of all personal data being processed.
  • Section 12 (Correction & Erasure): Right to rectify inaccurate data or order complete erasure without penalties.

🔒 Local-First Privacy Architecture

No Cloud Leak

Unlike traditional CRM or productivity apps that sell customer intelligence to advertising brokers, LifeOS is built on a Device-Hardware Isolated Model:

  • Private Room SQLite Database: Your task deadlines, revenue transactions, client contacts, and GST invoices reside strictly in your phone's sandbox.
  • Hardware Keystore Biometrics: Fingerprint and PIN lock are processed directly by Android's Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). The app never sees your raw biometric data.
  • Zero Analytics Brokers: No Facebook SDK, no ad tracking brokers, no hidden telemetry scrapers.

✨ AI Transparency & Google Gemini Integration

Model Disclosure

LifeOS uses Google's Gemini API for selective intelligence assistance (e.g. routine analysis, lead follow-up drafts, and sprint breakdown):

  • Zero Model Training: Your inputs are processed through secure API endpoints and are never used to train foundational AI models.
  • Selective Context: LifeOS only sends the specific prompt you ask. It never dumps your entire database to the cloud.
  • Offline Fallback: If you are offline, all task management, financial ledgers, and CRM actions work seamlessly without AI.
