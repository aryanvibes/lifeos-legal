📋 2. Official Privacy Policy & Architecture

Core Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the LifeOS mobile application for Android. LifeOS is built on a **Local-First Privacy Architecture**, meaning your data is stored locally in the application's isolated sandboxed directory on your smartphone.

A. Data Types Stored by LifeOS

  • Tasks & Habit Routines: Task titles, target timestamps, priority flags, and routine check-ins.
  • CRM & Sales Pipeline: Lead names, deal stages, deal values, and notes.
  • Financial Ledgers: Transaction amounts, GST/tax markers, and dates.
  • Operational Memory Vault: Encrypted strategic thoughts and personal goals.

B. Third-Party Sharing & Tracking

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. LifeOS contains no third-party ad networks, no analytics trackers (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel), and no automated data crawlers.

Gemini AI Query Routing:
When you explicitly ask LifeOS AI to assist with task planning or scheduling, only the prompt text you typed is transmitted over TLS 1.3 to Google Gemini API servers. Your broader SQLite database is never uploaded.

🔑 3. Device Permissions & Hardware Utilization

Play Store Disclosures

LifeOS follows the principle of Least Privilege. We only request permissions necessary to deliver your requested features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used strictly to notify you of task deadlines and routine alarms that you have scheduled.
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures your routine reminders fire at the exact minute without being delayed by battery optimizations.
  • VIBRATE: Provides tactile feedback during pomodoro timer completion and urgent task alerts.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Integrates with Android BiometricPrompt for optional app locking. LifeOS never accesses or stores your raw biometric templates.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly for verifying in-app subscriptions with Google Play Billing and sending user-initiated Gemini AI prompts.

📦 4. Data Portability & In-App Export

Zero Lock-In

Under Section 12 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and GDPR Article 20, you have the statutory right to export your complete personal workspace at any time in an open, machine-readable format.

How to Export Directly from the LifeOS Mobile App:
1. Open the LifeOS Android App.
2. Navigate to Settings → Data Sovereignty & Privacy.
3. Tap "Export Complete Workspace".
4. Select your format: JSON (for backups and cross-platform import) or CSV / Excel (for accounting and tax reporting).
5. The file is saved directly to your device's Downloads folder or shared via Android's native share sheet to your Google Drive.

Because LifeOS does not store your records on centralized remote cloud servers, the most comprehensive and immediate export is always generated directly on your device via the steps above.
